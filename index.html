Privacy Policy

LogSift - Your privacy is our priority

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to LogSift's privacy policy. This document explains how we handle your data when you use our application. We believe in complete transparency and want you to feel safe using our tools.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

LogSift is designed with privacy as the highest priority. We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (names, emails, phone numbers)
  • Log data or error content you analyze
  • Usage statistics or telemetry
  • Device information or identifiers
  • Location data
  • Any data transmitted to external servers

🔒 Complete Offline Processing

All log analysis happens entirely on your device. No log data, error messages, or any content you analyze is sent, uploaded, or transmitted to any external servers or third parties. The application operates in offline mode with no internet permissions required.

2. Local Data Storage

Within the application, you may choose to save analysis results locally for future reference. This data is stored:

  • Exclusively on your device
  • Using Android Room database local storage
  • Managed through the application's interface
  • Cleared whenever you choose to delete it

You have full control over saved data - you can view, rename, or delete any saved analysis at any time.

3. Third-Party Services

LogSift does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. The application is standalone and self-contained.

4. Children's Privacy

LogSift is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page. You are advised to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our privacy policy, feel free to contact us through the application's feedback feature or reach out via the project repository.

About LogSift

LogSift is a professional log analysis tool designed for developers, QA engineers, and anyone who needs to analyze error logs and stack traces. Built with Jetpack Compose and Material Design 3, the application provides comprehensive log parsing capabilities entirely offline.

  • Regex-based pattern matching for exceptions and stack traces
  • Frame categorization (Application, Android Framework, Third-Party)
  • Nested cause detection
  • History and sharing features
  • No internet permissions required
